    Cover Image Prompt

    Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook. This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

    Required specifications:

    • Format: PNG
    • Wide-landscape format
    • Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
    • This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

    The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color treatment, mascot, and title text.

    Subject & Tone

    Clocks and Watches with AI is a textbook about designing and programming real timekeeping devices, from simple LED clocks to round smartwatch displays, NeoPixel art clocks and Wi-Fi weather clocks, using MicroPython on the low-cost Raspberry Pi Pico. The intended audience is junior high and high school students who are new to programming and electronics. The visual tone should be playful, maker-friendly and modern-technical: exciting hands-on electronics, not a dry engineering manual.

    Title

    Place "Clocks and Watches with AI" in the center of the image, in a clean, highly legible bold sans-serif font. Use a white font color with a subtle drop shadow or a soft dark purple scrim behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage background. Keep the title large; do not shrink it to fit. Instead, simplify the background directly behind the text.

    Background Montage

    Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each rendered in the same flat vector illustration style so the composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

    1. Raspberry Pi Pico: a small green microcontroller board with a row of gold header pins and a USB port, plugged into a breadboard.
    2. Round smartwatch display: a glowing circular GC9A01 screen showing an analog clock face with tick marks and teal hour and minute hands.
    3. TM1637 LED clock: a red four-digit seven-segment display reading 12:00 with a glowing colon.
    4. NeoPixel ring clock: a circle of bright multicolored RGB LEDs, with lit pixels marking the hour, minute and second.
    5. Binary clock: columns of small glowing dots in on and off states, representing hours and minutes in binary.
    6. DS3231 real-time clock module: a small blue circuit board with a silver coin-cell battery.
    7. Wi-Fi time sync: Wi-Fi signal arcs and a small cloud with a clock icon, showing time arriving from the internet.
    8. Breadboard wiring: colorful jumper wires (red, black, yellow, blue) connecting a small OLED display to the board.

    Mascot

    Place the book's mascot, Chrono the Robot, in the lower-left corner, sized so it does not overlap the title text. Chrono is a small, chunky robot whose head is a round smartwatch display: a circular screen with a thin dark charcoal bezel and a small silver watch crown on the right side. A short antenna with a glowing teal tip sits on top of the head. The face is drawn on the screen in glowing teal lines: two large rounded eyes and a happy curved smile. The body is a rounded deep purple (#642580) box with a small teal (#41BAC1) gear emblem on the chest, two stubby arms with rounded mitten hands (one waving), and two short legs. The round head is about half of Chrono's total height. Draw Chrono in a modern flat vector cartoon style. A reference image is attached (docs/img/mascot/welcome.png).

    Style & Composition

    • Illustration style: modern flat vector with clean lines, bold simple shapes and minimal shading, applied to every montage element.
    • Color palette: deep purple (#642580) background gradient to a darker midnight purple, with bright teal (#41BAC1) accents and warm LED glows (red, amber and rainbow NeoPixel colors).
    • Lighting/mood: bright and optimistic, with a soft glow coming from the displays and LEDs.
    • Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring around it, Chrono in the lower-left, and generous negative space immediately behind the title so it stays readable.

    Avoid

    • Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
    • Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people pointing at whiteboards).
    • Avoid photorealistic human faces.
    • Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title.
