    Lab 04: Hello, Round Screen

    Time to wake up the screen. This lab writes "Hello World" in the middle and draws a thin ring just inside the edge of the glass.

    Welcome to Lab 04

    This is the screen every clock face in this kit is drawn on, so once it says hello, you're ready to build anything. My own face is a round screen just like it! Let's make time tick!

    What You Will Learn

    • how to start the display
    • how to write centered text in two font sizes
    • where the edge of a round screen really is

    Run It

    Open 04-display-test.py and click Run. You should see the picture above. The shell also tells you how long the display took to start.

    display = config.init_display()

display.fill(config.BLACK)
shapes.ring(display, config.CENTER_X, config.CENTER_Y, config.SAFE_RADIUS,
            config.CYAN, 2)

big = config.BIG_FONT
config.centered_text(display, big, "Hello", config.CENTER_Y - big.HEIGHT,
                     config.WHITE)
config.centered_text(display, big, "World", config.CENTER_Y, config.YELLOW)
config.centered_text(display, config.SMALL_FONT, "GC9B72  360 x 360",
                     config.CENTER_Y + 60, config.GRAY)
    • config.init_display() starts the display using the pins in config.py.
    • display.fill(config.BLACK) paints all 129,600 pixels black.
    • shapes.ring(...) draws a circle 2 pixels thick around the center.
    • config.centered_text(...) writes words centered left to right. The number after the words is how far down the screen they go.

    The Round Edge

    The display chip thinks its screen is a 360 × 360 square, but the glass is round. Anything drawn in the corners is never seen. The ring is drawn at config.SAFE_RADIUS, 168 pixels from the center: the biggest circle you can see all of. The clock faces in labs 08 and 09 place their ticks from that number.

    Check the ring first

    Look closely at the cyan ring. If any part of it hides under the edge of your display's case, make SAFE_RADIUS in config.py a little smaller now, and every clock face will fit.

    Try This

    1. Change config.YELLOW to config.color565(255, 128, 0) for orange.
    2. Move the words up by changing config.CENTER_Y - big.HEIGHT to config.CENTER_Y - 100. How high can text go before the round edge cuts it off?

    If It Doesn't Work

    • The screen stays black. Check that SCL goes to GP2 and SDA to GP3: swapping them is the most common mistake. Then check VCC on 3V3 and GND.
    • Snow or stripes. Check RST (GP4), DC (GP5), and CS (GP6). Long or messy wires can also do this: set BAUDRATE in config.py to 12_000_000.

    Hello, Screen!

    You started the display, centered text in two sizes, and found the real edge of a round screen. Every clock face from here on is built from those same steps.
