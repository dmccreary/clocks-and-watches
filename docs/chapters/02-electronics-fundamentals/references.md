# References: Electronics Fundamentals and Breadboard Wiring

1. [Ohm's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohm%27s_law) - Wikipedia - Covers the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance, with the formula, its history, and its limits. It supports this chapter's worked examples for sizing an LED resistor and calculating current.

2. [Breadboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breadboard) - Wikipedia - Explains the solderless breadboard, its rows and power rails, and how its internal metal strips connect component leads. It is useful background for the chapter's Breadboard Connectivity Explorer and wiring guidance.

3. [Light-emitting diode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-emitting_diode) - Wikipedia - Describes how an LED works, including polarity, forward voltage, colors, and the need for current limiting. It deepens the chapter's LED basics and LED current draw sections with real physics.

4. Make: Electronics (2nd Edition) - Charles Platt - Maker Media - Platt teaches by discovery: students run hands-on experiments first, such as deliberately overloading an LED, and meet the theory afterward. This order makes Ohm's law and current limiting memorable for beginners.

5. The Art of Electronics (3rd Edition) - Paul Horowitz and Winfield Hill - Cambridge University Press - No single author originated one idea here; this is the textbook most praised for clear intuition. Its rules of thumb and nonmathematical explanations of dividers and resistors suit the chapter's concepts.

6. [Ohm's Law Tutorial and Power in Electrical Circuits](https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/dccircuits/dcp_2.html) - Electronics Tutorials - Shows how to rearrange the Ohm's law formulas to find voltage, current, or resistance, and extends them to electrical power. It gives extra practice for this chapter's resistor-sizing calculations.

7. [How to Use a Multimeter](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/how-to-use-a-multimeter) - SparkFun Learn - Walks through measuring voltage, current, resistance, and continuity with a multimeter, including probe placement and safety. It directly supports the chapter's multimeter usage section and checking a circuit before powering it.

8. [Breadboards for Beginners](https://learn.adafruit.com/breadboards-for-beginners) - Adafruit Learning System - Explains breadboard layout, power rails, and how to place components and jumper wires, with clear photographs. It complements the chapter's breadboard wiring and wire color coding sections for first-time builders.

9. [Ohm's Law simulation](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/ohms-law) - PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder - An interactive simulation where students change voltage and resistance and watch current respond. It builds intuition for the chapter's Ohm's Law Explorer before they calculate by hand.

10. [Divider Circuits and Kirchhoff's Laws](https://www.ibiblio.org/kuphaldt/electricCircuits/DC/DC_6.html) - Lessons In Electric Circuits (Tony R. Kuphaldt) - A free textbook chapter on voltage dividers, how series resistors split a supply voltage, and the supporting circuit laws. It supports the chapter's voltage divider and voltage level sections.
